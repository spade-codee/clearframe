Welcome to ClearFrame

Save videos and audio to your PC, with quality up to 8K when the source offers it.

Click Next, then Install. Setup automatically downloads the required video tools from their official GitHub releases and checks their SHA-256 hashes. Keep your internet connection on. Allow roughly 150 MB of downloads and 1.5 GB of free space for setup; saved videos need additional space.

No terminal commands or administrator account are needed. After setup, open ClearFrame from the Start menu, paste a video link, choose Check link, Add to queue, then Start queue.

Installing an update keeps your library settings and downloaded videos. Close ClearFrame before installing. Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps; your download history and media are kept.
